# SS-01F Welcome Sign Evidence Cycle

This cycle completes the South Strip landmark evidence sequence at the semantic level.

Released:
- National Register identity record
- Historic design and lighting dossier
- Semantic sign model
- Median parking, walkway, crosswalk, signal, and road-context graph
- Source-backed parking-improvement history

Blocked:
- Survey-grade sign, foundation, parking-island, walkway, crosswalk, and signal geometry
- Terrain samples at approved origins
- Final sprite and 3D asset
- Survey-aligned and terrain-conforming placement

The resource is a historic roadside sign in public right-of-way, not a building. The pipeline therefore uses a dedicated sign-geometry gate and does not demand a building parcel or building footprint. Conflicting published street numbers remain preserved as an unresolved source conflict.
